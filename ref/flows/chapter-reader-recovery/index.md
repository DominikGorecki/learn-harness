# Current chapter history and corruption recovery

[Test implementation](../../../tests/desktop/topic-content-integration.spec.ts) / [Flow patterns](../../patterns-flow.md)

## Journey and assertions

Source and topic changes retain readable stale prose without inference. History focused on a removed section restores the current shorter immutable revision with heading focus and clamped scroll. A corrupt marker while away yields a recovery/topic-plan view without old chapter content; exact marker restoration and Reload recover current reading. Original text and media remain byte-identical.

## Evidence limits

Isolated Windows Electron and deterministic loopback fixtures qualify these process, bridge and recovery boundaries. They do not qualify live providers, educational quality, other platforms or packaged startup. This is a nonvisual flow with no fabricated screenshots.

# Outline generation and cancellation

Review explicit generation, readable saved outlines, topic disclosure, theme switching or cancellation.

[Test implementation](../../../tests/desktop/outline.spec.ts) · [Flow patterns](../../patterns-flow.md)

## Starting conditions

A temporary empty project and the signed local provider fixture returning the sample Bayesian reasoning outline. Pi runs in the actual Electron utility process.

## Journey and assertions

1. Enter a topic, follow connection recovery and verify authorization alone does not start inference.
2. Select the fixture model, generate and verify the saved outline and selected-model request; capture its overview.
3. Expand the first topic, assert the module heading and capture its content.
4. Change appearance and verify reading position and persisted outline remain unchanged without another request.
5. Request replacement, decline once, then start a held request and cancel it; verify draft/prior-outline preservation and worker exit.
6. Restart and reopen the saved outline without inference.

## Evidence limits

The provider response is a protocol fixture, not live AI output or evidence of learning mastery. The fullPage capture option does not expand the workspace internal scroll container.

Captures show Electron renderer content, without native title bars/menus. Any visible temporary absolute project paths are masked. Fixture account labels and learning text are synthetic. Visual references supplement the assertions and do not establish learning mastery or live-account access.

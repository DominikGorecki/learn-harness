## Journey and assertions

Actual Pi responses exhaust 48 turns with harmless list_materials calls. No accepted plan fails without resumable progress; an early validated plan pauses durably. Restart makes no inference request. Continuation uses the recorded model after the selected fresh-generation model changes and sends only missing stages. An edited source unique to progress disables continuation while the earlier chapter remains readable; explicit discard preserves original bytes.

## Evidence limits

Isolated Windows Electron and deterministic loopback fixtures qualify these process, bridge and recovery boundaries. They do not qualify live providers, educational quality, other platforms or packaged startup. This is a nonvisual flow with no fabricated screenshots.

# Account connection and restoration

Review account sign-in, credential renewal, model discovery or durable sign-out.

[Test implementation](../../../tests/desktop/account.spec.ts) · [Flow patterns](../../patterns-flow.md)

## Starting conditions

An empty temporary profile and the signed local ChatGPT provider fixture. The system-browser call follows the fixture URL; identity verification, callback, token exchange, IPC and storage use the real app.

## Journey and assertions

1. Open Account settings and complete fixture authorization; verify the connected panel and four model choices.
   Before authorization, check the empty AI activity bridge, named subscription/event stripping/unsubscribe, strict stale/extra-field cancellation rejection and forged-sender rejection. This is foundation bridge evidence; no AI producer/panel runs in this checkpoint.
2. Read the public account snapshot and check that access/refresh secrets are absent; capture the connected panel.
3. Expire only the isolated stored credential, restart Electron, and verify renewal without another authorization.
4. Sign out, verify credential removal and focus restoration, then restart and verify the disconnected state.

## Evidence limits

Assertions establish the local authorization/storage lifecycle, including the active OS credential adapter. They do not prove real-account entitlement, provider access or manual browser consent. Only the connected checkpoint is illustrated.

Captures show Electron renderer content, without native title bars/menus. Any visible temporary absolute project paths are masked. Fixture account labels and learning text are synthetic. Visual references supplement the assertions and do not establish learning mastery or live-account access.

# Folder material and clarification

Review folder-only setup, source coverage, clarification or unsupported-file recovery.

[Test implementation](../../../tests/desktop/materials.spec.ts) · [Flow patterns](../../patterns-flow.md)

## Starting conditions

Owned sample folders contain Markdown notes, unsupported PDFs, an excluded .env sentinel and ambiguous notes. A local provider fixture drives real Pi material-read calls.

## Journey and assertions

1. Connect the fixture account, open the notes folder and capture setup with an optional empty learning goal; opening sends no inference.
2. Create the outline and verify the saved reading heading separately from its dock preview, source references/coverage, exclusion of private sentinel content and byte preservation of the original notes.
3. Disclose source coverage, assert the supported source name and capture the result.
4. Open ambiguous notes, receive a clarification question, supply direction and verify it reaches the next request.
5. Open a PDF-only folder, verify editable recovery and no inference, disclose material considered and capture the unsupported-file state.

## Evidence limits

The sample PDF is intentionally unsupported. This flow establishes text/Markdown coverage and recovery through a fixture; it does not establish PDF ingestion or live-provider interpretation.

Captures show Electron renderer content, without native title bars/menus. Any visible temporary absolute project paths are masked. Fixture account labels and learning text are synthetic. Visual references supplement the assertions and do not establish learning mastery or live-account access.
